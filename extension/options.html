Sentral-Timetable-Extension-V2

Reads today's lessons (periods 1 to 4) from your Sentral timetable and sends them to your own Apps Script. Nothing else is read or sent.

Safety check: nothing is uploaded when you are looking at someone else's timetable. Leave blank to turn the check off.

Automatic scraping

Chrome must be open, and you must be signed in to Sentral. Requests from the phone app are checked every minute in the same hours.
