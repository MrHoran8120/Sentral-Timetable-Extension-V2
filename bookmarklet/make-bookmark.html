Sentral-Timetable-Extension-V2: bookmark version

For computers where Chrome will not let you install the extension. This page runs in your browser only; nothing you type here is sent anywhere. It makes a bookmark that reads today's periods 1 to 4 from your Sentral timetable page and sends them to your own Apps Script when you click it. It does not run by itself.

Nothing is sent if the page shows someone else's timetable. Leave blank to turn this check off.

1. Add it to your bookmarks bar

Drag this to your bookmarks bar (press Ctrl+Shift+B to show the bar):

Sync timetable

If dragging does not work: right-click the bookmarks bar, choose Add page, name it "Sync timetable", and paste the code below into the URL box.

2. Use it

Open your Sentral timetable (the current cycle), then click the bookmark. A small box appears in the corner showing what was sent. Your phone app's "Last scraped" time should change.

The bookmark contains your token. If your bookmarks sync to your school Google account, the token goes with them. It only lets someone read or replace your own timetable data, but treat it as private.
